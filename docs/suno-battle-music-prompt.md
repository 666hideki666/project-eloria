# Suno用バトル曲プロンプト

## 曲名

晶界を駆ける

英語表示を使う場合：

Race Through the Crystal Realm

## 推奨ファイル名

`battle-crystal-realm-v1.mp3`

## Suno Style欄

```text
Instrumental 1990s 16-bit fantasy JRPG battle theme, energetic and adventurous, original memorable lead melody, urgent but heroic rather than dark, sample-based orchestral game soundtrack, brisk 148 BPM, 4/4, D minor with brief Dorian brightness, punchy snare and toms, driving bass, rhythmic strings, bold but compact brass, agile woodwinds, harp accents, crystalline bell tones, subtle forest percussion, dramatic chord changes, clear melodic counterpoint, compact retro console mix, seamless gameplay loop feeling, strong opening hook, rising middle section, triumphant return, no fade-out
```

## Sunoへ入れる詳細プロンプト

```text
Create an original instrumental battle theme for a top-down fantasy action RPG set inside ancient forest-and-crystal ruins.

The music should evoke the excitement and melodic craftsmanship of a high-quality early-1990s 16-bit console RPG soundtrack without copying any existing composition, melody, arrangement, or game theme.

Open immediately with a short, unmistakable heroic hook. Use a fast but readable tempo around 148 BPM in 4/4. Build the track around a memorable original melody carried by bright strings and agile woodwinds, supported by compact brass stabs, driving bass, punchy snare, toms, timpani, harp accents, and small crystalline bell colors. Add subtle wooden and leaf-like percussion to connect the battle to an enchanted forest ruin.

The emotional tone is adventurous, courageous, mysterious, and exciting. It is the party’s first normal battle theme, so it should create momentum without sounding apocalyptic, tragic, evil, or like a final boss.

Suggested structure: a 4-bar opening hook, a 16-bar main battle section, a contrasting 16-bar rising section with a brighter harmonic color, a short tension bridge, then a strong return to the opening material. Make the ending reconnect naturally to the beginning for a seamless gameplay-loop feeling. Do not fade out.

Use a cohesive sample-based 16-bit orchestral palette with crisp retro console character. Keep the arrangement melodic and rhythmically active, but leave enough space for attack sound effects during gameplay.

No vocals, no lyrics, no spoken words, no modern cinematic trailer impacts, no EDM drops, no dominant electric guitar, no jazz soloing, no comedy tone, no horror ambience, no 8-bit square-wave chiptune lead, and no imitation of copyrighted game melodies.
```

## 短いプロンプト版

Sunoで詳細文が長すぎる場合はこちらを使用する。

```text
Original instrumental 1990s 16-bit fantasy action-RPG battle theme for ancient forest and crystal ruins. Energetic, heroic, mysterious, melodic, 148 BPM, 4/4, D minor with Dorian brightness. Sample-based orchestral game soundtrack with driving strings, agile woodwinds, compact brass, punchy snare and toms, harp, crystalline bells, subtle wooden percussion, strong memorable opening hook, contrasting rising middle section, seamless loop feeling, no fade-out. Leave space for combat sound effects. No vocals, no EDM, no trailer music, no dominant guitar, no copied game melodies.
```

## 生成時の目安

- Instrumentalを選ぶ。
- まず2〜4曲生成し、冒頭10秒で戦闘へ入りたくなる曲を優先する。
- 美しいだけでテンポが弱い曲より、短いイントロですぐ主旋律へ入る曲を選ぶ。
- 大音量の低音や常時鳴り続けるシンバルは、効果音を邪魔しやすいので避ける。
- ループさせたい部分に長い無音、余韻、フェードアウトがある曲は避ける。
- 採用曲はSunoからダウンロードし、`assets/audio/music/`へ保存する。
