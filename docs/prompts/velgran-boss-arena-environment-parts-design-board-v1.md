## Prompt

Create a polished environmental parts design board for Project Eloria's first boss arena, matching the attached underground ancient-ruins boss-arena concept and the exact visual language of the reference assets.

OUTPUT TYPE
- One landscape bitmap concept/design board, approximately 3:2 aspect ratio.
- A clean 3 columns × 2 rows layout: six separate environment panels divided only by narrow dark charcoal gutters.
- No written labels anywhere. No title, captions, letters, numbers, UI, characters, monsters, boss, health bars, logos, watermark, or checkerboard.
- This is an environment asset design board, not a playable screenshot and not a sprite animation sheet.

ART DIRECTION
- Highly polished top-down / slight three-quarter 16-bit JRPG pixel art.
- A cavern-deep ancient stone ruin, never outdoors and never on a mountain or exposed high place.
- Match the reference palette: charcoal-black cavern void, weathered olive-gray stone, muted moss green, dark warm-brown ancient roots, restrained luminous cyan crystal accents.
- Crisp intentional pixels, limited 16-bit-style palette, richly textured but gameplay-readable.
- Every object must share the same camera angle, pixel density, apparent tile scale, lighting direction, and world scale.
- Lighting comes from cyan crystals and faint ancient runes; no sunlight or open sky.
- Preserve large clear silhouettes and obvious collision footprints.

SIX PANELS
1. PLAIN COMBAT FLOOR KIT
   A broad uncluttered ancient stone floor sample for the center of the fight: medium rectangular and irregular masonry, subtle cracks, sparse moss, very faint cyan seams. Include several compatible floor variations without a visible editor grid. Keep contrast low enough for characters and attack effects to remain readable.

2. CIRCULAR RUNE-RING FLOOR KIT
   Curving pieces of the arena's concentric ritual circles: straight-ish arc segments, inner and outer ring pieces, cracked variants, and a small diamond rune junction. Thin restrained cyan inlay, weathered stone, seamless visual continuity. It must look like floor decoration, not a raised wall.

3. OUTER CAVERN BOUNDARY KIT
   Clear impassable arena-edge pieces where ancient masonry merges into cavern rock and thick roots: one straight section, one inside curve/corner, one outside curve/corner, and a broken section. Tall dark back edge, roots gripping stones, occasional tiny cyan crystals. The walkable side must be unmistakable; the void side must be deep black.

4. CRYSTAL PYLON KIT
   Compact ancient cylindrical stone pylons topped with luminous cyan crystals: intact version, cracked/damaged version, and a short ruined base. Roots and moss lightly wrap the bases. Each footprint must be compact and clear, suitable as a collision object near the arena edge, not oversized.

5. BROKEN COLUMN AND RUBBLE KIT
   Several edge-decoration ruins: a broken upright stone column, a fallen column segment, low rubble clusters, and a root-entangled ruined corner. Keep pieces modest in size and avoid shapes that would clutter or block the central battle zone. Clear dark contact shadows directly beneath grounded objects.

6. ENTRANCE AND BOSS-ALTAR TRANSITION KIT
   A wide open bottom entrance threshold leading from a narrow cave corridor into the circular arena, plus a low one-step altar rim/approach that echoes the attached Velgran sealing altar. Include ancient masonry, subtle cyan inlay, roots framing the sides, and an unobstructed passage. No closed gate, no sealing barrier, no boss, no characters. The altar is low and readable, not a tall stage.

COMPOSITION AND PRESENTATION
- Present each panel like a premium professional game-environment design board.
- Dark neutral gutters, balanced spacing, no decorative frame that resembles UI.
- Each panel should show enough surrounding floor or void to explain how the part connects.
- Avoid duplicated panels; every panel has a distinct practical purpose.
- Avoid excessive glow, bloom, fog, noisy particles, huge roots across walkable space, oversized lakes, or visual clutter.
- Do not imitate any named game or copyrighted character; keep Project Eloria's original world design.
