## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Project Eloria in-game boss-introduction cinematic storyboard
Primary request: Create a polished six-panel pixel-art storyboard showing the complete entrance and awakening sequence for Forest Crystal Beast Velgran in the attached underground ancient-ruins boss arena. Preserve the arena layout and the official character and boss designs from the references.

BOARD FORMAT
- One landscape image in a clean 3 columns × 2 rows layout.
- Six equal cinematic panels separated by narrow dark charcoal gutters.
- Each panel is a top-down/slight three-quarter 16-bit JRPG gameplay composition, not a close-up illustration.
- Maintain the same arena, scale, lighting, camera angle, and character proportions across all six panels.
- No panel labels, numbers, arrows, captions, dialogue boxes, UI, health bars, title text, letters, logos, or watermark.

OFFICIAL SUBJECTS
- Kyle: young human male traveler, gray hair, red-brown tunic, blue scarf, practical light clothes, long sword.
- Nadi: human female moonblade fighter with her official dark green/brown adventurer design and circular moonblade weapon.
- Mel: human female blue-haired mage in her official blue/orange adventurer mage design with a staff.
- Velgran: the official enormous sacred forest-and-stone guardian from the reference sheet, green mossy body, stone armor plates, root-like mane and horns, luminous cyan chest crystal. It is a holy guardian, not an evil demon.
- The three heroes must stay much smaller than Velgran, matching gameplay scale.

PANEL SEQUENCE
1. SOUTHERN ENTRY
   The camera frames the lower half of the arena. Kyle enters alone from the permanently open south corridor and walks onto the arrival apron. Nadi and Mel are not visible yet. The circular arena stretches into darkness above. Calm cyan ambient light; no combat effects.

2. PARTY FORMATION
   Kyle stops at the wide arrival apron. Nadi steps into position behind-left and Mel behind-right, forming a clear open triangular party formation on walkable floor. The entrance remains visibly open behind them. All three face north. No one overlaps a wall, root, pylon, or rubble.

3. CAMERA REVEAL
   The camera composition shifts north toward the altar. The three heroes are small at the bottom edge, while dormant Velgran lies curled on the low northern altar, still and dark. The central combat floor remains unobstructed. Crystal light subtly dims around the room.

4. CORE AWAKENING
   Closer but still gameplay-scale view of the northern altar. Velgran begins raising its head and forequarters; its cyan chest crystal and eyes ignite. Fine cyan light lines travel through the altar floor, tiny dust and pebbles lift, and old roots twitch. Sacred awakening, restrained and readable, not an explosion.

5. FULL RISE AND ROAR
   Velgran stands fully awake at the altar edge and roars toward the arena. Its enormous front claws are grounded, stone plates shift, the chest core shines brightest, and a controlled ring of dust, small stones, root tips, and cyan sparks spreads outward. Convey a brief camera shake without blur. Do not hide the boss silhouette with effects.

6. BATTLE START FRAMING
   Wide gameplay framing of the upper-central arena after the camera returns. Velgran occupies the north side of the combat circle, facing south. Kyle stands front-center, Nadi behind-left, Mel behind-right on the south side, all with clear spacing. The center remains open for dodging and boss attacks; the south retreat path remains visibly unobstructed. Strong battle-ready tension but no attacks have started.

ENVIRONMENT AND ART DIRECTION
- Use the exact underground ancient-ruins arena layout: circular weathered olive-gray floor, subtle cyan concentric rune lines, low northern altar, open southern entrance, cyan crystal pylons, thick ancient roots and broken masonry only around the outer boundary, deep black cavern void outside.
- Highly polished original 16-bit fantasy JRPG pixel art, crisp intentional pixels, cohesive limited palette.
- Lighting progresses from calm dim cyan to focused sacred cyan during awakening, then settles into readable battle lighting.
- Keep every central combat panel uncluttered.
- Characters and boss must be grounded with direct contact shadows, never floating.

STRICT CONSISTENCY AND AVOIDS
- Do not redesign the arena, boss, or heroes.
- Do not add extra characters, monsters, sealed gates, side exits, lakes, outdoor scenery, sky, cliffs through the arena, or central obstacles.
- Do not close or block the south entrance.
- Do not make Velgran malicious, skeletal, dragon-like, or mechanical.
- No modern cinematic letterboxing, no comic speech bubbles, no text, no imitation of any named game or copyrighted character.
